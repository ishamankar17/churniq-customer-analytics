
# ChurnIQ — Customer Churn Analytics & Prediction Platform

Customer churn analysis and prediction platform built using **Python, SQL, PostgreSQL, Scikit-learn, and Streamlit**.

## Analysis & Insights

* Analyzed **7,043 customer records** and identified an overall churn rate of approximately **26.5%**.
* Found that **month-to-month contract customers** have substantially higher churn compared with customers on longer-term contracts.
* Identified **short-tenure customers** as a high-risk segment, with churn decreasing as customer tenure increases.
* Analyzed churn across **internet service, payment method, contract type, monthly charges, and customer services** to identify high-risk segments.
* Examined customer spending patterns and service usage to understand their relationship with churn.
* Created features including **tenure groups, service count, average monthly spend, and high monthly charge indicators**.
* Used the analysis to generate **customer retention recommendations** for higher-risk segments.

## Machine Learning

Compared **Logistic Regression** and **Random Forest** for churn prediction.

## Application

Built a multi-page **Streamlit application** with:

* 📊 Churn Dashboard
* 🔎 Churn Analysis
* 🎯 Individual Churn Prediction
* 📈 Model Performance
* 💡 Business Insights & Retention Recommendations
